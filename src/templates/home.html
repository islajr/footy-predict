📖 System Usage Guidelines

1. Data Synchronization: Keep the database fresh. Smart Fetch is designed to conserve request limits on busy betting weeks. Click the Refresh Data button on the sidebar to check active calendars.

2. Interactive Drafting: The parlay builder is flexible. Original recommendations can be swapped for other markets in the dropdown list, but watch for the confidence indicators warning you of drops in probability.

3. Settle Your Slips: After matchweeks finish, navigate to the Parlay History screen and settle your slips to help train the weight calibrator.
